How it was created

Self-created to mirror the metrics an agency partnerships team tracks: revenue against JBP, pipeline, advertiser repeat rate and churn, formats live, ROAS and brand KPI delivery, and relationship health. Values were set so the portfolio spans all four health tiers and includes the patterns a partnerships lead must spot, such as an on-plan partner with weak ROAS. The 8 formats are illustrative: home screen banners, Food sponsored listings, Quik sponsored products, Rides placements, Careem Pay offers, Careem Plus member offers, push and CRM messages, and smart OOH screens on delivery bikes.
